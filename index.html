STAY SAFE

If you encounter a snake

Snakes are important to ecosystems. Give them space and let trained responders handle identification or relocation.

01

Keep your distance

Do not touch, corner, tease, or try to kill it.

02

Move away calmly

Warn others nearby and give the snake an escape route.

03

After a bite, get urgent medical help

Keep the person still and seek emergency care immediately. Do not cut, suck, ice, or apply a tight tourniquet.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Curiosity with care.

This beginner-friendly project introduces snake diversity in Kenya. Scientific names and ranges change as research improves. Verify every species against a current, reputable Kenyan checklist before submitting formal research.

Suggested references: National Museums of Kenya, Kenya Wildlife Service, IUCN Red List, and regional herpetology field guides.
